Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Sextant ("the app") is developed by Artem Demchuk, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter — profile, documents, certificate scans and photos, sea service records, contract dates — is stored only in the app's storage on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, we have no user accounts, and we cannot see, access or restore your data.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, contacts or crash data, and we do not track you across apps or websites. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Features that send data off your device — only when you use them

  • Document recognition (optional). If you enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings and scan a document, the image of that document is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's Claude API to read the fields. It is not sent to us. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). If you don't add a key, nothing is sent and you fill the fields manually.
  • Backup. A backup is a file encrypted on your device with AES-256 using a password only you know. You choose where to save it (e.g. iCloud Drive, Files, a messenger). We never receive the file or the password.
  • Sharing and links. When you share a CV or document, or open a map, website, email, WhatsApp or ITF help link, the data you choose to share goes to the app or service you pick, under that service's policy.

4. Device permissions

Camera and Photos — to add document scans and a profile photo. Face ID — to lock the app. Notifications — local reminders about expiring documents and contract end, scheduled on the device. You can revoke any permission in iPhone Settings.

5. Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase all data. Backup files you saved elsewhere remain under your control; delete them where you stored them.

6. Children

The app is intended for professional seafarers and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. If we ever add features that store data on our servers (such as an optional account), we will update this policy before they launch and ask for your consent where required.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Все данные — профиль, документы, сканы, стаж — хранятся только на вашем телефоне. У нас нет серверов с вашими данными, аккаунтов, аналитики и рекламы. Распознавание документов работает только если вы сами ввели ключ Anthropic API: тогда фото документа отправляется напрямую в Claude API (Anthropic), не нам. Резервная копия шифруется на телефоне вашим паролем, и хранится там, где вы её сохранили. Чтобы удалить все данные, удалите приложение. Полный текст — выше, на английском.
